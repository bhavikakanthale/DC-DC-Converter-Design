# DC-DC Converter Design and Simulation

## Project Overview
This project focuses on the design and simulation of a DC-DC power converter to understand voltage conversion, circuit behavior, and power electronics fundamentals.

## Objectives
- Understand the operating principles of DC-DC converters.
- Study component selection and design calculations.
- Develop a circuit schematic and simulate its operation.
- Analyze the output voltage and other relevant performance parameters.
- Document the design process and simulation results.
